SLAM Reference Template

Print this sheet at 100 %

Use US Letter, landscape, Actual size. Do not use “Fit to page”. After printing, lay a credit card inside the centre outline — if the edges do not line up, the print is scaled. Check the 6.00 in bar with a tape measure and, if it is not 6.00, enter that length in the Reference tab’s “Bar measured (in)” field.

On the Reference tab choose Printed template (Letter) — best, lay this sheet on the threshold line (any orientation), Capture, and tap the sheet.
